cells, primitive overrides and presence bits. Transplanting an older ordinary-
cell-only arena would bypass that contract. Its GC/root union must be retained.

The user-approved build process-group RSS envelope is 8 GiB, monitored over
the registered process groups with group cleanup on refusal. It is a sampled
guard, not an OS hard limit or proof of maximum RSS. Earlier 3 GiB refusals used
an uncalibrated assistant-selected limit and do not establish a refactor
regression. Stage time budgets and original package test populations remain
unchanged. Installed Homebrew reference results must be distinguished from
the three-switch source-built candidate; the installed reference lacks
multiple-threaded-vms and is not a matched speedup comparator.

Do not merge before the complete source-built compiler and both original
gerbil-mcp and gerbil-poo suites pass. Component success is not whole-package
acceptance. QNT owns temporal publication/invalidation/ownership histories;
Lean owns conditional value, layout, coverage and cost laws. This Makefile
dependency review adds no new temporal or mathematical proof.
